Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Prayers and Healings ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Prayer requests: the category and text you submit, and an optional email address if you'd like a private response.
  • Contact form messages: your name, email, and message when you reach out to us.
  • Basic visit data: we log page visits (URL and timestamp) to understand how the site is used. We do not collect names, emails, or precise location through this tracking.

How We Use Your Information

  • Prayer requests are reviewed before appearing publicly on the Prayer Wall. Only the category and prayer text are shown publicly, never your email address.
  • Contact form messages are used only to respond to you.
  • Visit data is used in aggregate to understand site traffic and is not shared with third parties.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not display third-party advertising on this site.
  • We do not share your prayer request's contact email publicly.

Data Storage

Information is stored securely on our hosting provider's servers. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no online system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

Your Choices

You can submit a prayer request without providing an email address. If you'd like a previously submitted request removed, contact us and we'll act on your request.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
